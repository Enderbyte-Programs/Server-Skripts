# Server Scripts

In order to make sure we are a 100% open source server, we, the administrators of the Enderbyte09 server have uploaded the scripts here.

## Information and Credits

### Historical Scripts

Playtime.sk - used for a brief period in 2026 after the 26.1 update but before the Unihome reforms. Programmed by Administrator M.

antievent.sk - used for building events, will probably be activated again. Programmed by Administrator M.

onduty.sk - The precursor for the AFK system, used from October 2 to October 16 2026. Programmed by Administrator R and Administrator M

survey.sk is deactivated and is only used when there is a survey available, which at the time of writing will be the Staff Satisfaction Survey in December 2026

### Current Scripts

afk.sk is the afkv2 system developed by Administrator M. It allows players to mark themselves afk or not afk

broadcast.sk is one of the oldest scripts in the system. Its purpose is to allow staff to send important messages. It was first developed by Former Administrator H in 2024 and has since been taken over and improved by Administrator M.

console.sk was written by Former Administrator H in 2024 and pretty much has not been changed since then. It is used for emergency powers by staff.

creative.sk prevents players from accessing the creative world that do not have permissions. It was developed by Former Administrator H and Administrator R in 2024.

discord.sk shows the discord link to players. It was developed in early 2025 by Former Administrator H.

firstjoin.sk handles all onboarding for new players. It was first developed by Former Administrator H in 2024 and underwent major improvements in 2026 by Administrator R.

hub.sk is a script made by Former Administrator H in 2024. It is synonymous to /lobby

invisitemframe.sk is a script that lets players get invisible item frames. It was written in 2026 by Administrator R

parkourclean.sk is an anti-cheating script made by Administrator R in 2026 after reports surfaced of illegal items in posession of players in Parkour world.

report.sk is a command that shows people the online support link. It was made in 2025 by Administrator R.

suicide.sk is a self-killing command written by Former Administrator H in 2024.
